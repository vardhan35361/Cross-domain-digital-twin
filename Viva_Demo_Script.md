# Viva Demo Script

1. Open the command center and point out the live core, five operational metrics, and alert ticker.
2. Orbit the 3D twin to show the extruded network, low-poly buildings, metro corridor, glowing road streams, and emergency dots.
3. Pause and resume the simulation; show the T+ tick and metrics moving through WebSocket telemetry.
4. Activate Rainstorm mode and explain the weather impact plus the rain layer.
5. Use AIRA: ask “Where is the bottleneck?” and show a streamed operational answer with confidence.
6. Walk through congestion forecasts, incident severity, and scenario lab.
7. Open `/api/docs` to demonstrate REST and WebSocket-ready backend design.
8. Run `docker compose up --build` and show Jenkins stages for a complete DevOps capstone story.
